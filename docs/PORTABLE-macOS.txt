x-reader for macOS 15 and later
Apple Silicon and Intel

Extract the ZIP, then move the entire x-reader Portable folder to a writable
location. Keep x-reader Portable.app and x-reader.portable together. Open the
app from that folder. If Finder isolates it in a temporary location, use
Open x-reader.command. The launcher opens the same signed app through macOS
Launch Services and supplies the location of your extracted folder.

This release is ad hoc signed and has no Apple Developer ID notarization.
If macOS blocks the first open, inspect the source and verify your download,
then follow Apple's instructions in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Your books, reading positions and notes are in data/library.sqlite3. Close
x-reader before moving or backing up its directory. To upgrade, replace the
app, launcher and notices with the new versions and keep the data folder.
